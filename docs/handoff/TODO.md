| Tag | Meaning | Who may set it |
|---|---|---|
| `[PENDING]` | Not started | anyone |
| `[IN_PROGRESS]` | Being worked on now (must match `STATE.md` "Active work") | owner |

| `[VERIFIED_COMPLETE]` | QA re-ran the acceptance test and it passed | **Subagent 7 only** |
